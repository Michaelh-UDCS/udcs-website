For Jehovah's Witness Families

Family Worship That's Meaningful—and Fun

Plan evenings that draw your family closer to Jehovah and keep everyone, especially the children, engaged and excited.

Sound familiar?

"What should we do for family worship?" Many family heads want something beneficial and relevant—that actually draws us closer to Jehovah—and that the kids look forward to. It's not always easy to come up with ideas that hit all three.

This app is designed to help: simple planning and interactive, Bible-based activities so your family worship is easier to prepare and more memorable for everyone.

Simple to use

No complicated setup. Choose a theme or type a topic that fits your family's needs. Get a tailored program with discussion points and activity ideas. Then add fun with Bible character games the whole family can play together.

Spiritual planner

Pick from suggested themes (courage, forgiveness, creation, prophecy, the fruitage of the spirit, and more) or enter your own topic. Get a clear, practical outline so you're never stuck wondering what to cover.

Game center

"Would You Rather" and "Guess Who" style games built around Bible characters. They're engaging for children and help the family connect Scriptural lessons with memorable, enjoyable time together.
